# Hitster Bingo

A browser companion for Hitster Bingo: disco-ball spinner, optional timer and digital scorecards on one shared screen.
The music still comes from scanning the original Hitster cards with the Hitster app.

## Play

Open the hosted page (GitHub Pages) on a laptop, tablet or phone in the middle of the table.

1. Add 2–6 players or teams, pick side A (easy) or B (hard), timer and joker frequency.
2. Tap the disco ball (or press <kbd>Space</kbd>) to spin.
3. Play the song, everyone answers.
4. Whoever was right taps a glowing box of that colour on their card.
5. **Next round ▶** – first full row, column or diagonal wins.

The game is saved in the browser after every action, so a reload picks up where you left off.

## Run locally

No build step – just open `index.html` in a browser.
